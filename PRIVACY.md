# Privacy Policy for Alpha|Beta

**Last updated: August 13, 2026**

Alpha|Beta ("the app") is designed to work entirely on your device, with no
data collection, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

## Data We Collect

We — the developer — do not collect, receive, or have access to any of your
data. Alpha|Beta has no servers, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs.

## Data Stored On Your Device (and Your iCloud)

The app stores the following information locally on your device, using
Apple's SwiftData framework:

- Your learning progress (which letters and words you've studied)
- Quiz history and scores
- Study streaks
- Your app preferences (selected language, color theme, pronunciation
  settings)

If you are signed in to iCloud, this data is synced across your own devices
using Apple's CloudKit, in a private database tied to your Apple ID. This
sync is end-to-end covered by Apple's iCloud privacy protections — the
developer cannot see, access, or receive this data at any point. See
[Apple's iCloud privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/icloud/)
for details on how Apple handles this data.

## No Advertising or Tracking

Alpha|Beta does not display ads, does not use tracking identifiers (IDFA or
similar), and does not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

## No Account Required

The app does not require you to create an account, and does not collect your
name, email address, or any other personal identifier.

## Children's Privacy

Alpha|Beta does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone,
including children. Since all data stays on-device and in the user's own
iCloud account, no personal information is ever transmitted to us.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same
location with a revised "Last updated" date.
